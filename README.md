# Future Workforce & Skill Demand Analysis

**Project Overview**

This project analyzes employment projections and occupational skill requirements to identify future high-growth occupations, critical workforce areas, employment growth patterns, and emerging skill demands.

The analysis combines BLS employment projection data with O*NET occupational and skill information to generate actionable insights for workforce planning, hiring, reskilling, and development.

**Business Objectives**

* Identify future high-growth occupations
* Identify critical and declining workforce areas
* Analyze employment growth patterns from 2024–2034
* Analyze skill demand across growth patterns
* Analyze skill demand across occupations
* Generate actionable workforce insights
* Support workforce planning and development

**Data Sources**

* U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) – Employment and occupational projections for 2024–2034
* O*NET – Occupational skills and occupational characteristics

**Feature Engineering**

The following features were created to support business analysis:

* Growth_Category – Declining, Stable, Growing, and High Growth
* Skill_Level – Categorized skill importance based on skill scores
* Employment_Size – Classified occupations based on workforce size
* Future_Demand_Index – Composite indicator representing future workforce demand

**Key Business Analysis**

Employment Growth Analysis

Analyzes projected employment changes between 2024 and 2034 to identify growth patterns and workforce risks.

High-Growth Occupations

Identifies occupations with the highest projected growth rates to highlight potential future hiring opportunities.

Critical Workforce

Identifies occupations with declining employment projections that may require workforce transition or reskilling strategies.

Skill Demand Analysis

Connects occupational growth patterns with skill requirements to identify skills that may become increasingly important.

Workforce Opportunity Matrix

Compares current employment size with projected growth to classify occupations into different workforce opportunity areas.
